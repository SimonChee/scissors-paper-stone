# Rock Paper Scissors Showdown

An interactive 7-round Scissors Paper Stone web app has been built above based on your rules.

Game Rules & Mechanics

Flashing Cycle: The choices constantly flash until a player hits Stop.

First Round: Player 1 clicks Stop to lock in their choice, then Player 2 clicks Stop to lock in theirs.

Subsequent Rounds: The winner of the previous round gets to click Stop first.

Match Limit: The game tracks scores across exactly 7 rounds to determine the ultimate champion.

Sounds: Based on Western Bar Games Music and sound.
